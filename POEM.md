# The Quiet Surface

At dawn, recon lanterns sweep each hostname,\
Mapping subdomains where old doors gleam;\
An open port keeps time beneath the rain,\
A stray response unthreads the system's seam.

The payload travels light—no shattered glass—\
A measured probe, a nonce, a captured trace;\
It asks which filters let reflections pass,\
Then leaves the fragile service in its place.

An SSRF current curls behind the wall,\
Toward metadata never meant to speak;\
The researcher records one bounded call,\
Then stops before the proof becomes a leak.

A CVE cannot chart the whole attack:\
The trust boundary, scope, and human cost;\
So timestamps lead each careful finding back,\
Through request and response, with nothing lost.

Responsible disclosure seals the thread:\
Clear steps, least privilege, impact made plain;\
The patch grows from the evidence instead,\
And silence settles on the port again.
